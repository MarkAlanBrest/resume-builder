Since you haven't held a job yet, you'll write a paragraph describing the skills, techniques, and knowledge you gained through your trade school program. This is completely normal — many trade school students are entering the workforce for the first time.

Skills & Training Paragraph
Write a paragraph in your own words about what you learned in your program. Be specific and honest — this will be used to build the Skills section of your resume.

✏️ What to Write About

  • Tools and equipment you learned to use (name them specifically)
  • Techniques and skills you practiced in class or the shop
  • Safety procedures and industry standards you were trained on
  • Hands-on tasks you can perform on your own
  • Soft skills developed: teamwork, attention to detail, time management

⚠ Important: Be Accurate

Write only about what you actually learned in your program. Do not exaggerate or include skills you haven't been trained on. This paragraph will be used directly in your resume — accuracy is essential for your professional reputation.

💼 How to Write Strong Bullet Points

  • Start with an ACTION VERB: Maintained, Operated, Assisted, Performed, Inspected, Installed…
  • Be specific: Name the tools, equipment, or tasks — "Operated hydraulic lifts and diagnostic scan tools" is much stronger than "Used equipment"
  • Skip "I": Write "Performed oil changes" — not "I performed oil changes"
  • Add detail: "Completed oil changes, tire rotations, and brake inspections on 8–10 vehicles per shift" tells the full story

Examples: Maintained a clean, organized work bay following shop safety procedures. | Assisted lead technician with engine diagnostics and component replacement. | Operated POS system to process customer transactions and returns.
