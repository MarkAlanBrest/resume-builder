✏️ How to Write a Strong Resume Bullet

Every bullet on your resume should answer: What did you do, how did you do it, and what was the result? Use this formula for each bullet point.

The Bullet Formula
Action Verb + Specific Task + Tools, Detail, or Number
❌ Weak
  • Helped customers
  • Used tools
  • I was responsible for cleaning
  • Worked hard every day
✅ Strong
  • Assisted 30+ customers per shift with product selection and returns
  • Operated hydraulic lifts, scan tools, and tire mounting equipment
  • Cleaned and sanitized work bays following OSHA safety procedures
  • Stocked inventory across 8 departments and rotated expired products
1
Start with an action verb. Never begin with "I" or "Was responsible for." Use words like Performed, Operated, Maintained, Assisted, or Processed.
2
Name real tasks and tools. "Operated POS register and handled cash transactions" is stronger than "worked the register."
3
Add a number when you can. Even a rough estimate helps — "8–10 vehicles per shift" or "served 40+ customers" shows scale.

As you write your bullets, you'll see live tips under each line. After you submit, you'll get feedback showing what each bullet could look like when polished for a resume.
